**AI Overviews** are the AI-written summaries that now appear at the top of many Google searches. **AI Mode** is a separate, chat-style way of searching Google where you ask follow-up questions. Both build their answers from web pages and other information Google has, and both show links to sources.

For small businesses, the short version is: **people still need to hire someone, and Google still needs to show them who.** AI changes how the answer is presented, but the businesses that get mentioned are mostly the ones that already do the basics of local SEO well. This guide explains what's changing and what to do about it.

## What AI Overviews and AI Mode actually do

- **AI Overviews** appear for some searches, usually questions ("how often should I service my boiler?") or comparisons. They summarize an answer and link to the pages used.
- **AI Mode** lets people have a longer back-and-forth with Google Search, such as planning a project or comparing options, again with links.
- **Local results haven't gone away.** For searches like "electrician near me", Google still typically shows the map and Business Profiles, because people want a business they can call.

Google explains how site owners fit into these features in its guide to [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## How it affects small businesses

### Informational searches: fewer clicks for simple answers

If someone searches "how to unblock a sink", the AI Overview may answer it on the spot. Pages built only to answer quick questions can lose clicks. That's a real change for blogs and DIY guides.

### Buying searches: still about you

When someone is ready to hire, they need a name, a phone number, reviews and confidence. That comes from your **Google Business Profile**, your **website** and what others say about you. AI answers can mention businesses, but they're drawing on the same information.

### Research before buying: a new chance to be mentioned

Bigger decisions ("is a heat pump worth it in the UK?", "composite vs timber decking") increasingly start with AI answers. If your website explains these clearly, it can be one of the sources linked.

## What Google says you need to do

Google's guidance is direct: there are **no special requirements or special optimizations** needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. The same SEO best practices apply ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)). In practice:

1. Your pages must be **indexed** and eligible to show a snippet in normal search.
2. Content should be **helpful, reliable and made for people**, as described in Google's guide to [creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
3. Important information should be in **text**, not only in images or PDFs.
4. Your **Business Profile** and site details should be accurate and consistent.

## 7 ways to stay visible as search changes

### 1. Make your Google Business Profile excellent

For local businesses this matters more than ever, because it's where the call happens. Complete every section, add real photos, and keep reviews coming. Start with our [Google Business Profile setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-business-profile/).

### 2. Give each service its own clear page

AI answers and normal search both reward pages that clearly answer one need: "Heat pump installation in Bristol", with what's included, how it works and real FAQs.

### 3. Answer the questions customers ask before they buy

Price factors, how long jobs take, which option suits which home, what can go wrong. Write these from your experience. Generic answers are exactly what AI summarizes away; specific, experienced answers are what it links to.

### 4. Show real experience

Photos of your work, named reviews, case studies with details, your licences and years in business. Google's guidance puts weight on experience and trustworthiness.

### 5. Collect reviews that mention the service and area

Reviews like "fixed our boiler in Leeds the same day" help both people and machines understand what you do and where.

### 6. Get mentioned on other trusted sites

Local news, trade associations, supplier "find an installer" pages and reputable directories. Mentions build the prominence Google uses for local rankings.

### 7. Track calls and enquiries, not just traffic

If traffic to your how-to articles dips but calls hold steady, you're fine. Watch your Business Profile performance (calls, direction requests, website clicks) and enquiries alongside Search Console.

## Can you see AI Overview traffic?

Google counts clicks and impressions from AI features within the normal Search Console Performance report, under the "Web" search type, rather than as a separate report, according to its [AI features documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features). So you can't isolate AI Overview clicks, but your overall Search Console data still reflects them. Our guide to [setting up Google Search Console](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-search-console/) gets you started.

## Can you stop your content appearing in AI Overviews?

You can use the same snippet controls (such as `nosnippet`) that limit how your pages appear in Search, but that also limits how they appear in normal results. For most small businesses, that's a bad trade: you want to be found.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to pay to appear in AI Overviews?

No. There's no way to pay for placement in the AI-generated answer. Ads can appear in separate, labelled positions.

### Will AI Overviews replace my website?

No. People still visit websites to check a business before calling, see photos and read reviews. Your site needs to make that decision easy.

### Is "AI SEO" or "GEO" different from normal SEO?

You'll hear new terms like GEO (generative engine optimization). For a small business, the work is almost entirely the same as good local SEO: accurate profiles, clear pages, real reviews and mentions. Be wary of anyone selling a secret AI ranking trick.

### What about ChatGPT and other AI assistants?

They work differently from Google but rely on similar public information. See [how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT](/blog/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-chatgpt/).

## Summary

- AI Overviews summarize answers at the top of Google; AI Mode is a chat-style search
- Simple how-to searches may send fewer clicks; buying searches still lead to businesses
- Google says no special optimization is needed: the same SEO basics apply
- Focus on your Business Profile, clear service pages, experienced answers, reviews and mentions
- Measure calls and enquiries, not just website traffic
