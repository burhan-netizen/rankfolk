When a pipe bursts at 11pm, nobody asks a friend for a recommendation. They grab their phone and search **"plumber near me"** or **"emergency plumber"**, then call one of the first businesses they see. SEO for plumbers is about making sure that's you.

The good news is that plumbing SEO is mostly local SEO, and the biggest wins are free: a complete Google Business Profile, a website with a page for each service, and a constant flow of reviews. Here's how to do each one, with the details that matter for plumbing businesses.

## The searches plumbers need to show up for

Plumbing searches come in two very different moods. Your SEO needs to cover both.

| Mood | Example searches | What wins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | "emergency plumber near me", "burst pipe plumber", "24 hour plumber" | Google Maps results, accurate hours, phone number front and center |
| Planned | "water heater replacement", "bathroom plumbing renovation", "repipe a house cost" | Detailed service pages and helpful articles |

Urgent searches are decided in seconds, mostly from the map results. Planned searches involve more research, so your website does more of the work.

## Step 1: Nail your Google Business Profile

For "plumber near me" searches, the map results are the prize. Google ranks them on relevance, distance and prominence ([Google's explanation](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).

What to set up:

- **Primary category:** "Plumber". Add secondary categories only if true, such as "Drainage service", "Water heater installation service" or "Gas installation service" (exact category names vary by country).
- **Service-area settings:** most plumbers go to the customer, so hide your home address and list the areas you cover.
- **Hours:** if you really offer 24/7 emergency service, set your hours to show it. If you don't, don't pretend. An unanswered emergency call often turns into a one-star review.
- **Services:** list everything: blocked drains, leak repairs, water heaters, toilet repairs, gas work, bathroom installs, and so on.
- **Photos:** your van, your team, before-and-after jobs, tidy finished work.

New to this? Follow our [Google Business Profile setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-business-profile/).

## Step 2: Answer every call (seriously)

This isn't a traditional SEO tip, but it matters. Google wants to show businesses that satisfy searchers, and customers judge you on whether you pick up. A plumber who ranks first but misses half their calls loses those jobs to the next listing.

If you can't answer during jobs, consider a call answering service, a callback promise in your voicemail, or a simple text-back reply so the customer knows you'll respond.

## Step 3: Build a page for each plumbing service

A homepage that says "we do all plumbing" won't rank for "tankless water heater installation". Give your main services their own pages:

- Emergency plumbing
- Blocked drains and drain cleaning
- Leak detection and repair
- Water heater repair and replacement
- Toilet, tap and fixture repairs
- Bathroom and kitchen plumbing
- Gas fitting (where you're licensed)
- Sewer line and pipe replacement

Each page should explain the service in plain English, show real photos, cover common questions and make calling you one tap away. Mention your main service area naturally in the heading, such as "Blocked Drain Clearing in Christchurch".

## Step 4: Make your phone number impossible to miss

On a plumbing website, the most important element is the phone number. On a phone screen it should be:

- At the top of every page
- Tappable, so it dials straight away
- Repeated after each section of a long page
- Ideally in a sticky button that stays on screen as people scroll

Emergency customers won't fill in a form and wait. Planned-job customers might, so include a short form for quotes too.

## Step 5: Get reviews that mention the job

Reviews are a ranking factor and the main thing customers compare between plumbers. Reviews that mention the specific job ("fixed our leaking water heater the same day") help even more, because they show customers and Google what you actually do.

You can't tell customers what to write, but you can make it easy: text your Google review link right after the job, while they're relieved and grateful. See [how to get more Google reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) for scripts. Never offer a discount in exchange for a review; Google doesn't allow it.

## Step 6: Write helpful content for planned jobs

People planning bigger jobs research first. Useful topics for a plumbing website include:

- How to tell if your water heater needs replacing
- What to do while waiting for an emergency plumber
- How to find and turn off your main water valve
- Tank vs tankless water heaters
- Signs of a hidden leak

These articles help homeowners, build trust and show up for question searches. Each one should link to the matching service page.

## Step 7: Get listed in the right places

Get your business on the platforms customers and Google trust in your country, with identical name, address and phone details:

- Apple Maps (Apple Business Connect) and Bing Places
- Your trade association and licensing body listings
- Manufacturer installer directories (water heater and fixture brands often have them)
- Well known home service directories in your country

Also ask builders, property managers and real estate agents you work with to list you as a recommended plumber on their sites.

## What about Local Services Ads?

In the countries where it's available, many plumbers also use Google Local Services Ads, which appear above the normal results and charge per lead rather than per click. It can be worth it, especially while your organic rankings grow. We explain how it works in [Google Local Services Ads: are they worth it?](/blog/google-local-services-ads-worth-it/).

## Plumbing SEO mistakes to avoid

- **"Best Plumber Sydney" as your Google business name.** Use your real name. Keyword stuffing can get you suspended.
- **Claiming 24/7 when you're not.**
- **Fake locations** to appear in suburbs you don't operate from.
- **Stock photos of smiling models in overalls.** Customers can tell.
- **No pricing guidance at all.** You don't need a price list, but explaining what affects the cost builds trust.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do plumbers rank for "plumber near me"?

Mostly through a complete, verified Google Business Profile with the right category, accurate service areas and lots of genuine reviews. Distance from the searcher also matters, which is why you'll usually rank best close to your base.

### Should plumbers use Local Services Ads?

Many do, where available. They charge per lead and appear above normal results, which suits urgent plumbing searches. Track the cost per won job to see if they pay off.

## Plumber SEO checklist

- [ ] Google Business Profile verified, category "Plumber", service areas set
- [ ] Accurate hours, including emergency availability if you offer it
- [ ] All services listed on your profile
- [ ] Website with a page for each main service
- [ ] Tappable phone number at the top of every page
- [ ] Review link texted after every job
- [ ] Every review replied to
- [ ] A few helpful articles for planned jobs
- [ ] Listed with trade bodies, manufacturers and trusted directories

The plumbers who dominate their local area usually aren't doing anything secret. They're doing these basics better and more consistently than the plumber down the road.
