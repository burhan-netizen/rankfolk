The best way to respond to a negative Google review is **quickly, calmly and briefly**: thank them, acknowledge the problem without arguing, apologize where it's fair, and offer to sort it out offline. Your reply isn't really for the unhappy customer. It's for the hundreds of future customers who will read it.

A thoughtful reply can turn a one-star review into proof that you handle problems well. A defensive one can cost you jobs for years. This guide gives you a simple formula, ready-made examples and what to do when a review is fake.

## Why replying matters

- **Future customers read replies.** They want to see how you behave when something goes wrong.
- **Google recommends it.** Replying to reviews is one of Google's own tips for improving your local presence ([source](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)).
- **It can fix the relationship.** Some customers update their review after a good response. Google notes that customers can edit their review after you reply ([source](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050)).

## Before you reply: three rules

1. **Wait until you're calm.** Never reply angry. Draft it, walk away, read it again.
2. **Check the facts.** Is this a real customer? What actually happened?
3. **Keep it short.** Three to five sentences is plenty.

## The 5-part formula for replying to a bad review

1. **Thank them** for the feedback.
2. **Acknowledge** their experience, without arguing about details.
3. **Apologize** for what's fair (you can be sorry they had a bad experience without admitting to everything).
4. **Explain briefly** what you're doing about it, if relevant.
5. **Take it offline** with a name and a way to contact you.

Never include the customer's private details, invoice numbers or job specifics they haven't shared publicly.

## Example responses you can adapt

### Late or slow service

> Hi James, thanks for letting us know, and I'm sorry we were late for your appointment. That's not the standard we aim for, and I understand how frustrating it is when you've arranged your day around it. We've changed how we schedule morning jobs to stop this happening again. If you're happy to, please call me directly on [number] so I can make it right. Sarah, Owner

### Unhappy with the quality of work

> Hi Priya, I'm sorry the finish on your patio isn't what you expected. We want every customer to be proud of the result. I'd like to come and take a look in person and talk through the options. Please give me a call on [number] or reply to our last email and we'll book a time. Daniel, [Business Name]

### Complaint about price

> Hi Mark, thanks for the feedback. I understand the final cost was more than you expected, and I'm sorry that wasn't made clearer at the start. We always aim to explain any changes before the work goes ahead. I'd be glad to go through the invoice with you. Please call me on [number]. Tom

### Rude staff member

> Hi Elena, thank you for telling us. I'm sorry you felt you weren't treated with respect. That's not how we want anyone to feel, and I'm looking into it with the team. I'd really appreciate the chance to hear more. Please contact me at [email]. Grace, Manager

### One-star review with no text

> Hi Chris, we're sorry to see you weren't happy with your experience. We'd really like to understand what went wrong so we can put it right. Please contact us on [number] when you have a moment.

### A review you don't think is from a real customer

> Hi, thanks for your review. We take all feedback seriously, but we can't find any record of working with you under this name. If you were a customer, please contact us on [number] so we can look into it.

Keep this one polite and factual. Future customers will judge your tone, not the reviewer's.

## What not to do

- **Don't argue in public**, even if you're right.
- **Don't share private details** about the customer or job.
- **Don't copy and paste the same reply** to every review. It looks automated.
- **Don't threaten** legal action in a reply.
- **Don't offer money or discounts to remove or change a review.** Google's policies prohibit offering incentives to revise or remove negative reviews ([Google's policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114)). You can still fix a genuine problem for the customer; just don't make it conditional on the review.

## How to reply on Google

You need a verified profile to reply. Then, per [Google's help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050):

1. Go to your Business Profile and open **Reviews**.
2. Select the reply icon next to the review.
3. Write your reply and submit.

Google checks replies against its policies, which usually takes about 10 minutes but can take up to 30 days. Your reply appears publicly under the review as a reply from the business, and the customer is notified. You can edit or delete your reply later.

## What to do about fake or unfair reviews

You can report a review, but Google will only remove it if it **breaks Google's policies**. Disagreeing with a review isn't enough ([Google's guide to reporting reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773)).

Reviews that may qualify include:

- Spam or fake reviews (for example, from someone who was never a customer)
- Reviews that are off-topic or about a different business
- Conflict of interest, such as a competitor or ex-employee
- Offensive, hateful or harassing content

### How to report a review

1. In your Business Profile, open your reviews.
2. Select the report option next to the review.
3. Choose the reason that fits and send the report.

You can check the status in Google's **Reviews Management Tool**. Google says evaluation typically takes several days. If a review you reported isn't removed, the tool allows a one-time appeal.

Always reply publicly too, in the calm style above. Even if the review stays, your reply shows readers the other side.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I delete a bad Google review?

No, businesses can't delete reviews. You can report a review that breaks Google's policies, and Google removes it if it agrees. Otherwise, the best response is a calm public reply and more genuine reviews from happy customers.

### How fast should I reply to a negative review?

Within a day or two is ideal. Quick replies show you're attentive, but don't rush a reply while you're upset. A thoughtful response the next morning beats an angry one in five minutes.

### Should I reply to old negative reviews?

Yes, if they're still visible. Future customers read old reviews too. A short, professional reply, even late, shows you take feedback seriously.
