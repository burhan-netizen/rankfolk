HVAC SEO means making sure your heating and cooling business shows up when someone's AC dies in a heatwave or their furnace or boiler fails in winter, **and that they can call you in one tap.** For most HVAC companies the biggest wins are a complete Google Business Profile, a page for each service and system type, steady reviews, and planning content around the seasons.

What makes HVAC different from other trades is **seasonality**: demand for cooling and heating swings through the year. Good HVAC SEO prepares for each peak before it arrives.

## The searches HVAC companies need to show up for

| Type | Example searches | What wins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency repair | "AC repair near me", "furnace not working", "no heat emergency" | Business Profile, answered phone, reviews |
| Maintenance | "AC tune-up", "boiler service [town]", "furnace maintenance" | Service pages, maintenance plan page |
| Replacement | "new AC unit cost", "heat pump installation [town]" | Detailed service pages, financing info |
| Research | "heat pump vs furnace", "how often to service AC", "why is my AC blowing warm air" | Helpful guides |

Terms vary by country: "furnace" and "AC" in the US and Canada, "boiler" and "air conditioning" in the UK, "split system" and "ducted" in Australia. Use the words your customers use.

## Step 1: Make your Google Business Profile work hard

- **Primary category:** the closest match to your main work, such as "HVAC contractor", "Air conditioning contractor" or "Heating contractor".
- **Services:** list repair, installation and maintenance for each system type you handle.
- **Service areas:** the towns you really cover.
- **Hours:** reflect emergency availability honestly, and update holiday hours.
- **Posts:** use Business Profile posts for seasonal reminders ("Book your AC tune-up before summer").
- **Photos:** real installs, your team, vans and clean finished work.

See our [Google Business Profile setup guide](/blog/how-to-set-up-google-business-profile/) for every section.

## Step 2: Build pages by service and system

Create separate pages for your main services, for example:

- AC repair / AC installation
- Furnace or boiler repair / replacement
- Heat pump installation
- Ductless mini-split or split system installation
- Maintenance and service plans
- Indoor air quality (if you offer it)
- Commercial HVAC (if you offer it)

Each page should explain common problems, what the visit involves, typical timeframes, what affects cost, brands you install or service, and FAQs. If you offer financing or rebates help, explain it clearly, linking to official rebate or incentive sources for your area rather than quoting amounts that change.

## Step 3: Plan content around the seasons

Publish or refresh seasonal content **a month or two before** each peak, so Google has time to pick it up:

- **Before summer:** "Why is my AC blowing warm air?", "AC tune-up checklist", "Signs you need a new AC"
- **Before winter:** "Furnace or boiler not turning on: what to check first", "How often should a boiler be serviced?"
- **Shoulder seasons:** heat pump guides, replacement planning, maintenance plans

Simple safety-first troubleshooting guides ("check the thermostat and breaker before you call") build trust and often lead to calls when the easy fixes don't work.

## Step 4: Make calls and bookings easy

- Tap-to-call number at the top of every page
- A short booking or quote form
- Clear emergency information
- A maintenance plan sign-up page, if you offer plans (recurring customers are worth a lot)

## Step 5: Turn every job into reviews

Ask after every visit, especially emergency repairs, when customers are most grateful. Reviews that mention the system and town ("fixed our AC in Phoenix the same day") help both trust and relevance. Our [review request generator](/tools/review-request-generator/) makes it quick.

## Step 6: Get listed where HVAC customers look

- Manufacturer dealer or installer locators for brands you're certified with
- Trade associations and licensing registers
- Apple Maps, Bing Places and reputable directories

## HVAC SEO mistakes to avoid

- Waiting until the heatwave to fix your website
- One generic "Heating and Cooling" page for everything
- Out-of-date hours and holiday hours on your profile
- No mention of brands, certifications or warranties
- Unanswered phones during peak season

## Frequently asked questions

### When should HVAC companies start seasonal SEO?

A month or two before each peak. Updating pages during the rush is too late for them to rank.

### Do HVAC companies need a blog?

Not a busy one. A handful of genuinely useful seasonal and troubleshooting guides, refreshed each year, is enough for most local companies.

### How do I rank in more towns?

Start with your Business Profile service areas and mention the towns naturally on your service and contact pages. Only create separate town pages if each has genuinely useful local content; copied pages with the town swapped don't help.
